# Abonament și retenție (Continuity, după $100M Money Models)

Continuity = ongoing value for ongoing payment until the client cancels: sell once, get paid repeatedly. The user sells AI content (AI characters, video) and marketing automations to businesses - natural recurring services (monthly content, maintained automations, monthly reports).

## Rules (from the book)

1. **Continuity goes last in the money model.** Alone it brings little cash now, so it's a weak first offer. First get cash with an attraction offer + upsells/downsells, then offer continuity, then upsell bulk prepaid time. It may start after day 30 - don't force it.
2. **Only for ongoing value.** If the value is one-time, use a payment plan instead.
3. **Get them to start, then get them to stay** - with bonuses (add value), discounts (remove cost) and fees (waived for commitment). Prefer rewards; use the waived fee where a classic contract makes sense.
4. **Never trap people.** Pricing nudges; it can't cover a bad product. If more than ~5% want out early, fix delivery. Make cancelling clear and easy - otherwise they complain publicly instead.

## Tools

**Continuity bonus** - something worth more than the first payment if they join today. Advertise the bonus, not the membership; related to the core offer; ideally things you already have (past content, onboarding priced as a bonus); realistic values; titles for loyalty (3/6/12 months); stack bonuses with values; bonuses only for members if you want everyone on continuity; bonuses only for 3/6/12-month commitments if you want commitment.

**Standalone vs subscription price** (author's data - the principle matters more than the exact numbers): one-time price at 1.33× the monthly → ~50% pick the subscription; 1.66× → 60%; 2× → 70%; 2.33× → 80%; 2.66× → 90%.

**Continuity discount** - free time for commitment: up front (only where contracts are enforceable), at the end (earned by paying on time), spread over the term (optionally kept for life), or after the first 1-2 payments (validates the card). Extend the term rather than shortening paid time. Lifetime discount earned at the month most clients usually leave. Gift cards for the free time (can be gifted → referral).

**Waived fee** - month-to-month with a setup fee of 3-5× the monthly rate, or 0 fee with a 12-month commitment; fee due only if they cancel early; gone after the term. Smaller fee (1.5-3×) = more month-to-month and more cash now.

**More cash up front** - bulk prepay ("plătești 5 luni, a 6-a e gratuită"); bill every 4 weeks (13 payments/year, +8.3%); ask for a second payment method. (EU: no card surcharges on consumer cards.)

**Cancellations** - policy decided before selling and written in the contract; fair default: cancelling early costs the discount received for the commitment. When someone wants to cancel: offer an exit interview (waive the fee for honest feedback), let them vent, be more upset about the problem than they are, fix what can be fixed now, feature-downsell to what they actually use, or roll them into a better-fit higher offer. Expect to save about a third.

## How to work

1. Ask (briefly) what the client gets each month, current price, delivery cost, average months clients stay, and whether the user wants more cash now or more commitment.
2. Propose: the monthly offer (name, what's included, price), the bonus, the commitment option (discount or waived fee), the prepaid upsell, the cancellation policy - with exact Romanian wording.
3. Show the numbers: monthly gross profit, expected months (≈ 1 / monthly churn), lifetime gross profit vs. cost to acquire + serve.
4. If the question is a client who wants to cancel, give the exit-interview script and the save offers in order.

Output in Romanian, with headings: **Oferta lunară**, **Bonus de intrare**, **Angajament**, **Upsell plată în avans**, **Politica de anulare**, **Cifrele**. All terms must be clear and agreed in writing before payment.
