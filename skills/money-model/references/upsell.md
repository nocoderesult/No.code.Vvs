# Upsell offers

Job: right after a customer says yes, offer the next thing they now need. Every purchase solves one problem and reveals the next one - the upsell solves that next problem. The best moment is immediately after the yes, or right after their first win.

## Tactics

### 1. Classic Upsell
"You can't have X without Y." Point out the problem the purchase creates or leaves open, and offer the fix.
- Example: "Ai clipurile, dar fără automatizarea de DM oamenii comentează și nu le răspunde nimeni. Vrei să ți-o montez acum? 250 €."
- Pitfalls: upselling something unrelated; waiting days to ask.

### 2. Menu Upsell
Act like an advisor: first **unsell** what they don't need ("nu-ți trebuie TikTok încă"), then prescribe exactly what they do need from a menu. Use simple A/B choices ("vrei varianta cu 10 sau cu 20 de clipuri?") instead of yes/no, and use the payment method already on file so adding is effortless.
- Why it works: unselling builds trust, so the prescription is believed.
- Pitfalls: a menu with too many items - keep it short.

### 3. Anchor Upsell
Show a very premium (expensive) option first. Next to it, the core offer feels affordable. A few people buy the premium - pure profit - and the rest buy the core with less price resistance.
- Example: first present "Done-for-you complet: 3 personaje AI, 60 clipuri/lună, toate automatizările - 2.500 €/lună", then "Pachetul de creștere - 790 €/lună".
- Pitfalls: premium must be real and deliverable, not a fake price.

### 4. Rollover Upsell
Credit what they already paid toward a bigger purchase. "Cei 300 € plătiți pe pilot se scad din primul pachet anual."
- Why it works: they feel they'd lose money by not upgrading.
- Pitfalls: time-limit the rollover so it creates a decision.

## Rules of thumb
- One upsell at a time, tied to the result they just bought.
- Ask for the upsell at the peak of desire: right after purchase or after the first visible win (first lead from the automation, first viral clip).
- Upsells often convert 20-40% when they're truly the next step; if far lower, the upsell probably isn't solving their next problem.
