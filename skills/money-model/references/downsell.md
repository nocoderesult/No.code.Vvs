# Downsell offers

Job: when a customer says no, don't let them leave with nothing. A "no" is usually a no to *this price, this size, or this risk* - not to the result. Find which one and offer a smaller yes in the same conversation.

## Tactics

### 1. Payment Plan Downsell
Same offer, payments spread out. Try in order: full price in a few installments (optionally with a small fee for splitting), then more installments, then a free/low-cost start with payments beginning later.
- Example: "Nu e problemă - îl poți plăti în 3 rate de 270 € în loc de 790 € acum."
- Pitfalls: plans so long that delivery costs come before the cash; no card on file / no contract.

### 2. Trial With Penalty
Let them start free, but tie the trial to conditions (attend the onboarding, send materials, use the system). If they meet them, it stays free; if they don't, a pre-agreed fee is charged.
- Why it works: lowers risk for the customer while making sure only committed people take the free trial - and committed people get results and buy.
- Pitfalls: the penalty and conditions must be explicit and agreed in writing before the trial starts.
- Example: "7 zile gratuit. Condiția: ne trimiți poze cu produsele în 48h și participi la apelul de setup. Dacă nu, se taxează 100 €."

### 3. Feature Downsell
Lower the price by removing features, quantity, speed or personal attention - never by cutting the core result. Offer it as "a version that fits now".
- Example: from "personaj AI + 20 clipuri + automatizare" (790 €) to "doar 8 clipuri cu un personaj din librăria noastră, fără automatizare" (290 €).
- Pitfalls: removing so much the customer won't get a result (then they churn and complain); making the lower tier so good nobody buys the higher one.

## Rules of thumb
- First find the real reason for the no (price? timing? trust? size?) - ask, don't guess. Match the downsell to it: money → payment plan; risk → trial; size → feature downsell.
- Downsell once or twice, then stop - endless discounting destroys trust and price.
- People who take a downsell and get results are later upsell candidates.
