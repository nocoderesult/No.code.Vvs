# Downsell offers (Stage II - turn no into yes)

A downsell is whatever you offer after a no. You change **how they pay** (payment plan, trial) or **what they get** (quantity, quality, features) - never the price of the same thing.

## Rules of downselling
- They said no to *this* offer, not to you. Use the no to learn what they actually want.
- Downsells are trades: if you give something, take something.
- Personalise, don't pressure: more of what they like, less of what they don't, matched with price.
- Offer the same things in new ways - don't create a hundred products.
- **Never drop the price of the same thing** to close. That's discounting, it destroys trust ("was he ripping me off on everything else?"), and customers talk. Planned price tests are fine; panic discounts are not. A different, cheaper thing is fine.

## 1. Payment Plan Downsell - "How much can you put down today?"
Same product, same total; only timing changes. Go from most cash up front to least, stop when they buy:
1. Show the full price, then a discount for paying in full (frame: the plan price includes "interest"; prepay saves it) - reward paying in full, don't punish paying over time.
2. Third-party financing, credit card ("vrei să alegi tu termenii? atunci folosește cardul de credit"), or layaway (pay in instalments, get it when fully paid).
3. Half now, half on next payday (or "cât poți da azi?" and the rest on payday).
4. Temperature check: "Pe o scară de la 1 la 10, cât de mult vrei asta?" 8+ → continue ("găsim o soluție"); 7 or less → "ce ar face să fie 10?" and move to a feature downsell.
5. Thirds: 1/3 now, 1/3 on each of the next two paydays/months.
6. Even payments over the service period (e.g. weekly).
7. Trial With Penalty.
- Seesaw version (fewer steps): "Vrei rate lunare mari sau mici?" → "mici" → present prepay with big discount and zero monthly payments → if no, the more they put down the smaller the instalments.
- Built-in upsell: periodically offer to pay off the balance and still get the original prepay discount (pay the salesperson for it too).
- Schedule charges on paydays; retry declines later the same day (recovers ~1/3).
- Check: total closes go up while the paid-in-full share stays the same - otherwise you're putting full-payers on plans.
- Start high: annual billing churns far less than monthly.

## 2. Trial With Penalty - "If you do X, Y, Z, I'll let you start free"
- Only as a downsell, never as the first offer. Present the paid offer first.
- Free as long as they follow the terms; they pay only if they don't. Use the same criteria as Win Your Money Back (actions/results that make great customers). Give more rather than less.
- Always take a card (and ID). If they won't give a card and commit to the work, don't sell them.
- Sell staying and paying: "Dacă îți aduce rezultatul, rămâi pe termen lung?" No → no trial.
- Explain how the fees motivate (they're there to keep them on track). One big fee vs small fee per missed action - per-action is usually better.
- Make check-ins required - they are the upsell meetings.
- After the trial: liked it → offer longer/higher version; disliked → take the blame, understand, offer the better-fit higher offer; didn't use it → reach out, waive the fee if they meet with you (a small fee isn't worth a 1-star review).
- Let them make up missed actions before charging. Call it simply a "free trial".
- Best for recurring services where the customer must do work for results (use Pay Less Now / Pay More Later for products and one-time services). "Prima lună 1 €" makes taking a card easier.
- EU: all fees and criteria written and signed **before** the card is taken (the book has customers initial each fee clause - do that, upfront).

## 3. Feature Downsell - "Why don't we try this instead?"
Lower the price by changing what they get: less quantity, lower quality, cheaper alternative, or remove a whole feature (including the guarantee).
- Every feature has a price and a value. Remove features **from highest value to lowest**: the first downsell makes them reconsider the original offer; later ones find the best deal for them.
- Formula: remove something, lower the price, ask "Cum ți se pare acum?"
- Service quality levers: hours/days available, response speed, delivery speed, 1-1 vs group, channel (text/chat/video), who delivers (owner/senior/junior), live vs recorded, DIY / done-with-you / done-for-you, personalisation, guarantee length/coverage/terms.
- Last resort: done-for-you → do-it-yourself product; or a free orientation that ends with a DIY offer.
- Never negotiate price for the same thing. Want to pay less now → payment plan; want to pay less overall → feature downsell.
- Stay a helpful guide, not a pushy seller. Standardise the downsell order once you know what customers value.
- Name the combinations (top = aspirational status name; cheapest = "Minimum": "deci nimic peste pachetul minim, corect?").
- After two downsells, do the 1-10 check; 8+ → payment plan downsells; alternate the two.
- After each downsell ask "Facem așa?" / "E corect?"
- Downsell current customers who don't use features before they cancel.
- Barter: discount in exchange for reviews, a video testimonial, progress posts, two introductions. (EU: reviews must be genuine and the incentive disclosed.)
