# Continuity offers

Job: turn one-time buyers into recurring revenue. Continuity is where long-term profit lives - each month a customer stays adds profit without new acquisition cost. The tactics here make people *start* and *stay*.

## Tactics

### 1. Continuity Bonus Offer
Give a valuable bonus for joining the recurring plan - ideally something they receive immediately and that increases their success (so they stay).
- Example: "Dacă intri pe abonamentul lunar de conținut, primești gratuit un al doilea personaj AI pentru campaniile sezoniere."
- Pitfalls: bonus that costs a lot to deliver; bonus unrelated to the outcome.

### 2. Continuity Discount Offer
Reward longer commitments: free months or a lower price for paying or committing for longer up front.
- Example: "Plătești 6 luni deodată și a 7-a lună e gratuită." / "Contract pe 12 luni: 590 €/lună în loc de 690 €."
- Why it works: cash up front funds acquisition; longer commitment means time to get results (and less churn).
- Pitfalls: discounts so deep the margin disappears.

### 3. Waived Fee Offer
Charge a real setup/start fee, and waive it if they commit to the recurring plan. If they cancel before the agreed period, the fee becomes due.
- Example: "Crearea personajului AI și setup-ul automatizărilor costă 600 €. Dacă semnezi pe 6 luni, taxa e 0. Dacă renunți mai devreme, se plătește taxa."
- Why it works: the fee is genuinely the cost of setting up; waiving it makes the commitment feel like a win, and the fee discourages early cancellation.
- Pitfalls: must be in the contract, clearly, before signing.

## Rules of thumb
- Recurring offers must deliver ongoing value every month (new content, maintained automations, reports) - otherwise people cancel no matter the tactic.
- Track churn (% who cancel per month). Average lifetime ≈ 1 / monthly churn; lifetime gross profit should comfortably exceed CAC.
- In the EU, auto-renewal and cancellation terms must be clear and cancellation must be possible as easily as signing up.
