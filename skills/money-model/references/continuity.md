# Continuity offers (Stage III - Get the most cash)

Ongoing value for ongoing payment until they cancel: sell once, get paid again and again. Continuity alone is a weak attraction offer (little cash now), so put it **last**: attraction, upsell, downsell first; then continuity; then upsell bulk prepaid time. Only make things continuity if the value is ongoing (a one-day workshop is a payment plan, not continuity).
Three tactics: bonuses (add good stuff), discounts (remove cost), waived fees.

## 1. Continuity Bonus Offer - "If you liked that, you'll love what's next"
- Give something awesome if they sign up today, usually worth more than the first payment.
- Advertise the bonus, not the membership. Keep bonuses related to the core offer (wrong bonuses attract wrong customers).
- Use things you already have/do (past content, onboarding - price it and make it the bonus). Physical bonus for digital products, digital bonus for physical ones.
- Realistic values only - ideally products you already sell, at their real prices.
- Titles as bonuses (silver/gold/diamond after 3/6/12 months).
- Free bonus and steep discount are interchangeable framings.
- Anchor with the bonus value, then: "Vrei să afli cum îl poți primi gratuit?" → join today; stack more bonuses with their values.
- Bonuses only for members if you want everyone in continuity.
- Standalone vs continuity price (author's data): standalone 1.33× continuity → ~50% choose continuity; 1.66× → 60%; 2× → 70%; 2.33× → 80%; 2.66× → 90%. Closer prices = more standalone buyers.
- More cash up front: also sell bonus-only standalone (1.33-2.66× the first month); upsell bulk prepaid time ("plătești 5 luni, a 6-a e gratuită" - 1 in 8 taking it lifts 30-day profit ~50%).
- Want commitment? Give bonuses only to those who commit 3/6/12+ months (fewer sales, more committed customers).

## 2. Continuity Discount Offer - "Sign up today, get X time free"
- Free time in exchange for a commitment. Apply it 4 ways: **up front** (only where contracts are enforced; delays cash), **at the end** (earned by paying on time), **spread over the term** (e.g. 600 / 12 = 50 off monthly, optionally for life if always on time), **after the first 1-2 payments** (first + last month, last month up front, or activation fee - also validates the card).
- Bill every 4 weeks instead of monthly: 13 cycles a year, +8.3% revenue for the same work.
- Extend the term instead of eating into it (12 paid + 3 free = 15 months).
- Get a second payment method (EU: no card surcharges to consumers; frame the benefit differently).
- Gift cards for the free time - usable later or gifted to a friend (referral).
- Lifetime discount earned at your most common churn month.
- Cancellation: decide the policy up front; simplest fair version - cancelling early costs the discount received in exchange for the commitment. Make cancelling easy and clear (otherwise they complain elsewhere). Ask for an exit interview (waive the fee for honest feedback) - you save ~1/3, or roll them into a better-fit offer.

## 3. Waived Fee Offer - "Month to month with a setup fee, or commit for a year and I waive it"
- Setup fee for month-to-month, usually 3-5× the monthly rate; waived if they commit (12 months minimum, best for slow-result services); due if they cancel early; gone once the term is completed.
- Bigger fee → more commitments; smaller fee (1.5-3×) → more month-to-month and more cash up front.
- Justify: "Ne costă să te pornim. Dacă vrei doar să testezi, acoperi tu costul; dacă te angajezi pe termen lung, îl acopăr eu."
- If more than 5% want to cancel early, fix the product - pricing nudges, it must never trap people in something they dislike.
- Example: abonament conținut + automatizări 690 €/lună; setup 2.000 € la lună-la-lună sau 0 € pe 12 luni (datorat doar la renunțare înainte de termen).
